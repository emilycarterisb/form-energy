Funding timeline

  • 2021 – Series D – about $240M closed, with ArcelorMittal’s XCarb fund among investors; the chemistry was disclosed.
  • 2022 – Series E – US$450M raised, taking total investment to around $800M.
  • 2024 – Series F – $405M to move to serial production; the round was led by T. Rowe Price.
  • August 2026 – Series G – $750M, again led by T. Rowe Price, bringing total equity to more than $2B at a reported valuation of about $2.5B.

Proceeds are earmarked for scaling manufacturing in Weirton and advancing commercial deployments. The company also appointed Wes Sloan, a veteran of aerospace, automotive and energy, as Chief Operating Officer.

Why the backlog matters

Form reported that its backlog grew roughly fourfold, from about 20 GWh to 80 GWh, during 2026. The AI data centre boom is a major driver: developers need firm, clean power and are willing to sign multi-gigawatt-hour deals. A large backlog helps the company justify manufacturing expansion, but backlog is not revenue, and conversion depends on delivery schedules and regulatory approvals.

Competitors and alternatives

  • Ore Energy (Netherlands) uses the same reversible iron oxidation concept, targets about 100 hours and raised $43M in a Series A in August 2026.
  • Elestor promotes a hydrogen-iron flow battery with 75%+ round-trip efficiency claims.
  • Lithium-ion remains cheaper and more efficient for short durations, and is increasingly stretched toward longer durations.

Risks and what to watch

  • Efficiency: low round-trip efficiency limits economics where energy prices are high.
  • Execution: ramping serial manufacturing is hard for any first-of-a-kind chemistry.
  • Regulation: utility projects such as Georgia Power’s need commission sign-off.
  • Proof at scale: long-run field data from Minnesota will matter.
Bottom line: Form Energy has the capital, customers and a factory. The next milestone is demonstrating reliable multi-day performance in the field at scale.

Frequently asked questions

How much has Form Energy raised?

Reports put total equity funding above $2 billion after a $750 million Series G in August 2026.

Who are Form Energy’s biggest customers?

Announced agreements include Xcel Energy (for a Google data centre), Crusoe, Georgia Power and Great River Energy.

Is Form Energy’s backlog the same as revenue?

No. Backlog represents contracted or committed capacity yet to be delivered, and conversion depends on execution and approvals.

Does Form Energy have competitors?

Yes. Ore Energy uses similar iron-air chemistry, and other long-duration technologies such as flow batteries compete.
